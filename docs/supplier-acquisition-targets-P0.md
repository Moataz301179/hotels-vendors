# P0 Supplier Acquisition Targets — HotelsVendors INVO
**Priority 0 Categories | Phase 1 Launch (Months 1–6) | Version 1.0**

---

## Executive Summary

This document defines the **P0 (highest priority) supplier acquisition targets** for HotelsVendors INVO marketplace launch. These categories represent the **critical path** for onboarding anchor hotel chains (Orascom, Jaz, Pickalbatros) — without suppliers in these categories, hotels cannot place their first orders and the platform has zero utility.

**Target: 50 suppliers across 5 P0 categories by Month 4** (10 per category minimum for viable marketplace liquidity).

---

## P0 Category Definition Framework

A category is **P0** if it meets **ALL** of:
| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Order frequency** | Daily/weekly (not monthly/quarterly) |
| **Spend concentration** | Top 20% of hotel procurement budget |
| **Compliance urgency** | ETA e-invoicing mandatory + high audit risk if non-compliant |
| **Supplier fragmentation** | Many small/medium vendors (no dominant incumbent) |
| **Anchor chain demand** | Explicitly requested by Orascom CPH / Jaz Procurement |

| Step | Action | Owner | SLA | INVO Automation |

| 4 | **ETA Compliance Setup** — Digital cert, invoice template, test submission | Compliance | 24h | Sandbox test → production cert |
| 5 | **Factoring Eligibility** — AI scorecard, credit limit, funder match | Payme Engine | 2h | Real-time scoring on first invoice |
| 6 | **Logistics Profile** — Warehouse location, vehicle type, coverage map, SLAs | Operations | 24h | GIS integration, route optimization |
| 7 | **Contract & KYC** — Framework agreement, VAT responsibility, AML | Legal | 48h | DocuSign + auto-generated annexes |
| 8 | **Go-Live** — First test order from pilot hotel | All | Day 1 | End-to-end tracking |

## Risk Mitigation (P0 Specific)

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Ecolab/Diversey refuse marketplace** (channel conflict) | High | High | Sign Al-Masria/Delta as primary; Ecolab as "premium tier" later |
| **Mahalla/Kafr El Dawwar require LC payment** | Medium | High | Payme factoring = supplier paid in 24h, hotel pays at 60 days |
| **Coastal suppliers lack cold chain** | Medium | High | INVO logistics partners (refrigerated vans) + shared routes |
| **SME suppliers can't generate ETA invoices** | High | Medium | INVO generates on their behalf (delegated cert) — zero tech required |
| **Import-dependent suppliers (coffee, spices) face FX risk** | High | Medium | Price in EGP with quarterly review clause; hedging via bank partners |
| **Anchor chains demand exclusive supplier contracts** | Medium | High | Platform = non-exclusive; but offer "preferred supplier" badge + priority placement |
