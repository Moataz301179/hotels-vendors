## Slide 5: ETA E-Invoicing — Native Compliance

**Every invoice issued through the platform is automatically submitted to the Egyptian Tax Authority.**

What this means for your finance team:
- **No manual ETA portal uploads.** Invoices are digitally signed and UUID-tagged at generation.
- **Real-time validation.** ETA status tracked per invoice: PENDING → VALIDATED → ACCEPTED.
- **Dead-letter queue handling.** Failed submissions retry automatically. Manual resolution path available.
- **Audit-ready records.** Immutable invoice history with before/after state snapshots.

**This is not an add-on. It is the compliance backbone of every transaction.**

## Slide 6: Embedded Factoring

**Your suppliers get paid in 24 hours. You keep your 60-day terms.**

How it works:
1. Invoice is issued and ETA-validated
2. Factoring partner reviews risk (hotel credit score, invoice amount, trust tier)
3. Supplier receives funds within 24 hours at a discount rate
4. Hotel pays the factoring partner on original due date

**Key terms:**
- **Non-recourse.** The supplier has zero default risk. The factoring partner prices risk into their discount rate.
- **No balance sheet impact.** The factoring facility is between the supplier and the factor.
- **No extra paperwork.** Integrated at checkout. One toggle.
