## Post Body

The Egyptian SME financing gap is EGP 39 billion.

Traditional banks serve 600 large corporates. The other 219,000 SMEs? They wait.

Oliv is changing that with digital factoring. HotelsVendors is changing procurement.

But here's what nobody talks about:

The real problem isn't access to finance. It's access to VERIFIED transactions.

A supplier with a confirmed purchase order from a 5-star hotel is a better credit risk than most bank borrowers. Yet traditional finance ignores them.

HotelsVendors + Oliv solves this:

→ Verified orders = verified invoices
→ Verified invoices = instant credit
→ Instant credit = faster fulfillment
→ Faster fulfillment = happier hotels

The data IS the collateral.

This is what embedded finance looks like.
