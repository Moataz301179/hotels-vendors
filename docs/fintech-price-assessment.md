## CONCLUSION

The revised pricing model is **market-validated** and **conservative**:

1. **Subscription prices aligned** with Egyptian SaaS benchmarks (EGP 500-12,000/month)
2. **Platform fees competitive** with MaxAB-Cartona (1.0-1.75% vs 2-5%)
3. **Factoring commission realistic** (0.75% within 0.3-2.0% market range)
4. **Lower entry barrier** (EGP 2,500 minimum vs EGP 5,000)
5. **Stronger incentive** for factoring (43% discount vs 25%)

**The model is profitable from Month 1** because:
- LLM costs are negligible (Ollama = free, OpenRouter = $0.09/M tokens)
- Platform fees scale with GMV (no fixed costs)
- Factoring commission is 100% passive (Oliv does the work)
- Subscription margin is 70-90% (value-based pricing)

**Key correction:** STARTER tier at EGP 500 is unprofitable. Recommend raising to EGP 750 or limiting features to reduce cost-to-serve.
