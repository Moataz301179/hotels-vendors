## 1. Why this angle wins

- **High commercial/B2B intent:** F&B cost is the #1 controllable line in a hotel P&L. The searcher *is* the decision-maker (F&B Dir. / GM / Procurement Head).
- **Demonstrable pain:** Egypt is the world's #1 wheat importer, cooking oil is largely imported, EGP went ~16.7 → ~48.6/USD, headline inflation hit 35.7%. The "30% food cost" rule is fiction here. We expose and replace it with Egypt-correct math.
- **Competitive gap:** Competitors (Amazon-style marketplaces, generic F&B blogs) quote global benchmarks. None localize for FX, freight, spoilage, and ETA invoice timing. We own the localized calculator + a monetizable lead-capture tool.
