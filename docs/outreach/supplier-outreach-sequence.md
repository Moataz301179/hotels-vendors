## Expected Response Rates (honest benchmarks for cold B2B in Egypt)
- LinkedIn acceptance: 20-35%
- Email #1 reply rate: 5-10%
- Cumulative reply rate across 3 emails: 8-15%
- Reply → signup conversion: ~30-40%

For 100 qualified suppliers: ~8-15 replies, ~3-6 signups. The Oliv financing angle drives higher engagement than marketplace access alone — cash flow pain is the sharper hook.
