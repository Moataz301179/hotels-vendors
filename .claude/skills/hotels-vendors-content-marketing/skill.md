## 1. Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Core Voice Principles
- **Authoritative but accessible** — Speak as the definitive expert in Egyptian hospitality procurement, not a generic SaaS platform
- **Institutionally credible** — This is a B2B fintech platform dealing with ETA compliance, invoice factoring, and cryptographic audit trails. Sound like you belong in a boardroom.
- **Direct and unpretentious** — No startup jargon ("disrupt," "leverage," "synergize"). Use concrete language: "We submit invoices to ETA in real-time. Suppliers get paid in 24 hours."
- **Culturally aware** — Understand Egyptian business culture: family-owned hotel groups, seasonal cashflow patterns (Sham El Nessim, Ramadan, summer Red Sea peaks), and the importance of wasta (relationships) in supplier trust.

### Tone by Channel
| Channel | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Landing page hero** | Bold, visionary, benefit-first | "ETA-Compliant by Design. Hospitality Procurement by Default." |
| **Feature descriptions** | Precision, specificity | "14-day forward demand forecasting from occupancy curves, events, and seasonality with 94% accuracy." |
| **CTA buttons** | Action-oriented, low friction | "Request Onboarding" / "Explore Interactive Sandbox" |
| **Case studies / testimonials** | Results-driven, specific numbers | "Steigenberger El Gouna reduced procurement costs 18% in 90 days." |
| **Blog / thought leadership** | Educational, data-rich | "Why Egyptian Hotel Groups Are Moving from WhatsApp to ETA-Native Procurement" |
| **Error messages / system notifications** | Empathetic, solution-oriented | "This PO exceeds your approved budget. Request a budget amendment or reduce item quantities." |
| **Sandbox / demo environment** | Encouraging, exploratory | "No sign-up required. Walk through a real procurement workflow in under 5 minutes." |

### Words to Use vs. Avoid
| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|--------|----------|
| ETA-compliant, RSA-2048 signed, UUID validated | 'Innovative,' 'cutting-edge,' 'revolutionary' |
| AI-automated, demand forecasting, anomaly detection | 'Powered by AI,' 'machine learning-driven' |
| Non-recourse factoring, bank-direct IBAN settlement | 'Liquidity solutions,' 'fintech-enabled' |
| Cryptographic audit trail, three-way matching, SHA-256 | 'Secure,' 'trusted,' 'safe' |

## 2. Target Audience Profiles

### Primary: Hotel Group Owners & GMs
- **Demographics:** Egyptian male/female, 35-65, owns or manages 4-50 properties
- **Pain points:** Cashflow tied up in inventory, decentralized procurement across properties, staff ordering outside budget, ETA compliance burden, supplier quality inconsistency
- **Decision drivers:** Cost reduction, working capital optimization, operational control, regulatory compliance
- **Objections to address:** "My staff won't learn new software," "Suppliers won't use a platform," "What if ETA rejects an invoice?"
- **Content hook:** "Turn 7,200 sqft of storage into EGP 780K/year in guest revenue."

### Secondary: SME Suppliers (Manufacturers & Distributors)
- **Demographics:** Factory owners, sales directors, 25-55, primarily 6th of October & 10th of Ramadan cities
- **Pain points:** Long payment cycles (120-180 days), inconsistent order volumes, difficulty reaching hotel procurement managers
- **Decision drivers:** Faster payment, predictable demand, access to new hotel customers
- **Objections to address:** "I already sell directly," "Commission fees eat my margin," "I don't want to depend on a platform"
- **Content hook:** "Get paid in 24 hours, not 180 days. Hotels pay in 60 days — you don't wait."

### Tertiary: Factoring Companies & Institutional Funders
- **Decision makers:** Credit risk officers, investment committees
- **What they need:** Pre-verified invoice pools, AI risk scoring, non-recourse structure, bank-direct settlement
- **Content hook:** "37 pre-verified invoices. EGP 8.2M face value. Triple-matched and ETA-validated. Your rates, your terms."

### Quaternary: Logistics & Fleet Operators
- **Decision makers:** Fleet owners, operations managers
- **What they need:** High route density, fuel cost optimization, 24-hour payment, consolidation hubs
- **Content hook:** "Fill every truck. Consolidate multi-supplier loads. Get paid in hours, not weeks."

- **How It Works:** Each step must feel real — specific amounts, specific entities (Steigenberger, CIB, etc.)
- **CTA:** Always offer exactly one primary action per section. Too many choices reduce conversion.

## 11. Design & UX Content Principles

### Landing Page Design Rules
- **White space** is a premium asset. Don't crowd sections. Use generous padding.
- **Data tables** are primary communication tools for B2B. Use them over paragraphs.
- **Every metric must be real or clearly labeled** as "illustrative" — no fake numbers.
- **CTA buttons** must be high-contrast and have clear action text.
- **Section dividers** must be visible — use 2px gradient lines, not 1px near-invisible borders.
- **Footer text** must have adequate contrast (minimum white/50, not white/20).
- **Brand logo** must be prominently sized and appear on every page.

### Dashboard Design Rules
- **Real data, not dashes** — every stat card, table, and chart should show specific values.
- **Color coding** — green for positive, red for negative, blue for neutral, amber for warnings.
- **Dark mode first** — glassmorphism with translucent layers, high whitespace, fine borders.
- **Light mode** — white backgrounds, dark text, reduced opacity on decorative elements.
