How It Works

Four steps to a smarter supply chain.

Our RFQ-driven model ensures you always get the best price — no static catalogs, just competitive quotes.

01

Create RFQ

Submit your requirements — AI auto-fills specifications and suggests quantities based on historical data.

02

Receive Quotes

Verified suppliers compete. AI scores bids on TCP (Total Cost of Procurement), not just unit price.

03

Approve & Execute

Multi-level Authority Matrix validates. ETA e-invoice auto-generated. Order routed to logistics.

04

Settle & Factor

Supplier paid in 48h via embedded factoring. Full audit trail, reconciled to ETA.
