How It Works

Four steps from need
to delivery

Register, discover suppliers through RFQ matching, checkout with negotiated payment terms, and let suppliers get paid — factoring included.

Register Free

Create your hotel profile with tax ID, authority matrix, and procurement preferences. No credit card required.

Reverse Factoring

Liquidity that moves
at your speed

Suppliers get paid on shipment — not on your 30/60/90-day terms. Factoring companies bid to fund your payables, and you settle later at rates that reflect your credit.
