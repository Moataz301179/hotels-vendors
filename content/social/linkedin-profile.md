## About (2,000 chars max)
HotelsVendors is Egypt's B2B procurement orchestrator and fintech platform for the hospitality industry.

We connect coastal hotels with suppliers through a unified platform that automates procurement, ensures ETA e-invoicing compliance, enables reverse factoring (suppliers paid in 48 hours), and uses AI to forecast demand at 94% accuracy.

**What we solve:**
• Hotels lose 15–25% of procurement spend to waste, emergency orders, and manual processes
• Suppliers wait 30–60 days to get paid, tying up working capital
• ETA compliance is mandatory but manual compliance is error-prone and unscalable

**How we solve it:**
• AI-powered procurement forecasting — 94% accuracy across 50+ hotels
• Built-in ETA e-invoicing — digital signatures, UUID validation, real-time submission
• Reverse factoring — suppliers get paid in 48 hours, hotels keep Net-30/Net-60 terms
• Shark-Breaker shared logistics — 40% delivery cost reduction for Red Sea resorts

**Built for Egypt, by Egyptians.**
Headquartered in Cairo, serving hotels across Sharm El-Sheikh, Hurghada, and the North Coast.
