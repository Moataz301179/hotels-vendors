## Day 3 (Wednesday) — ETA Compliance Explainer
**Platform:** LinkedIn + Facebook (Arabic)
**Format:** Infographic + bilingual caption

**Caption (English):**
ETA e-invoicing is no longer optional in Egypt.

Every B2B transaction must be digitally signed, UUID-validated, and submitted to the Tax Authority in real time. Non-compliance penalties: EGP 50,000 – 500,000 per violation.

Hotels processing 200–500 invoices monthly can't afford manual compliance.

HotelsVendors makes it invisible:
✅ Digital signature at creation
✅ Real-time UUID from ETA
✅ SHA-256 audit trails
✅ Three-way matching (PO + delivery + invoice)
✅ Zero manual work

Compliance built into every transaction. From order to settlement. Forever auditable.

## Day 4 (Thursday) — Reverse Factoring Explainer
**Platform:** LinkedIn + Instagram (Reel/short video)
**Format:** 60-second explainer script + caption

**Script (60s):**
"Hotels want Net-60 terms. Suppliers want to be paid yesterday.

So who bridges the gap?

Reverse factoring.

Here's how it works on HotelsVendors:

Hotel places order — standard Net-30 terms.
Supplier ships goods.
Hotel confirms receipt.
Factor pays supplier in 48 hours — at a 2–4% discount.
Hotel pays factor on day 30 — original terms preserved.

Everyone wins. Suppliers get working capital. Hotels preserve cash flow.

And the rate? Based on the hotel's credit — not the supplier's. Because a 200-room resort in Hurghada is a better credit than a small linen supplier in Cairo.

That's supply chain finance. That's HotelsVendors."

**Caption:**
Suppliers get paid in 48 hours. Hotels keep Net-60 terms.

Reverse factoring unlocks working capital for both sides — without anyone losing.
