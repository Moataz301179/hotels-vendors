# HotelsVendors — Social Media Graphic Templates

## Design System
- **Background:** #000000 (OLED black) or #0A0F1A (dark navy)
- **Accent:** #a3e635 (lime green)
- **Text:** #FFFFFF (white), rgba(255,255,255,0.6) for secondary
- **Font:** Jakarta Sans (or Inter as fallback)
- **Border:** 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08) on cards
- **No gradients, no bolds** (brand consistency with website)

## Image Sourcing
- **Custom graphics:** Generate via imagegen-frontend-web (brand-consistent)
- **Hotel photography:** Use licensed stock (Unsplash) or original
- **Data visualizations:** Build with brand colors in Figma/Canva
- **Icons:** Lucide React icons, exported as PNG at 2x

## Tools
- **Canva Pro:** Quick social graphics using brand kit
- **Figma:** Custom illustrations and complex layouts
- **CapCut:** Video editing for Reels/TikTok
- **Buffer/Later:** Scheduling and analytics
