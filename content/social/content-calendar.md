# HotelsVendors — 30-Day Content Calendar

## Content Pillars (rotate weekly)
1. **Product** — feature deep-dives, how-it-works, demos
2. **Industry** — hospitality news, ETA updates, procurement insights
3. **Founder** — Moataz's journey, vision, behind-the-scenes
4. **Social Proof** — testimonials, metrics, early user stories
5. **Arabic** — Egyptian market content, local context

## Weekly Cadence

### Monday — Thought Leadership (LinkedIn)
Long-form post on hospitality procurement, fintech, or AI trends.

### Tuesday — Product Feature (All platforms)
Deep-dive into one feature: AI forecasting, ETA, factoring, logistics.

### Wednesday — Industry News (LinkedIn + X)
Commentary on Egyptian hospitality news, ETA regulations, B2B trends.

### Thursday — Visual/Video (Instagram + TikTok + Reels)
Short-form explainer, infographic, or behind-the-scenes.

### Friday — Founder's Note (LinkedIn)
Personal reflection, company updates, or team spotlight.

### Saturday — Community (Instagram Stories + Facebook)
Polls, Q&As, user-generated content, or fun facts.

### Sunday — CTA / Growth (All platforms)
Registration drive, partnership announcement, or weekly recap.

## Monthly Milestones
- **Week 1:** Launch announcement + founder story
- **Week 2:** Feature deep-dives (AI, ETA, factoring)
- **Week 3:** Industry commentary + Arabic content
- **Week 4:** Social proof + growth metrics + next month preview

## Content Mix
- 40% educational (how-to, explainers, guides)
- 25% thought leadership (opinions, trends, founder voice)
- 20% product (features, updates, demos)
- 15% community (engagement, UGC, team)

## Bilingual Strategy
- LinkedIn: 70% English, 30% Arabic
- Instagram: 50% English, 50% Arabic
- Facebook: 30% English, 70% Arabic
- X: 80% English, 20% Arabic
- TikTok: 40% English, 60% Arabic
