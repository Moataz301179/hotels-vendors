## Tone
- **Confident but not arrogant** — we're disrupting procurement, but we're doing it for the hotels
- **Bilingual** — English primary, Arabic for Egyptian audience (code-switch naturally)

## What We Are
Egypt's B2B hospitality procurement + fintech platform. We connect hotels with suppliers, automate ETA compliance, enable reverse factoring, and use AI to forecast demand.

## What We Are NOT
- Not another generic marketplace
- Not a food delivery app for hotels
- Not replacing your procurement team — we're arming them
