# How to Onboard Hotel Suppliers in Egypt: A Step-by-Step Guide

Egypt's hotel supply chain is fragmented. Thousands of suppliers — from F&B distributors to linen manufacturers — operate without standardized onboarding, digital invoicing, or verification. HotelsVendors changes this.

## Why Supplier Onboarding Matters

Before a supplier can sell to hotels on HotelsVendors, they must be verified. This protects hotels from:

- **Fraudulent suppliers** with fake commercial registries
- **Tax non-compliance** — suppliers who can't issue ETA-compliant invoices
- **Quality risk** — unverified suppliers with no track record
- **Delivery failures** — suppliers without logistics capability

## The 4-Step Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Document Submission (15 minutes)

The supplier submits:
- Commercial Registry (السجل التجاري)
- Tax Card (البطاقة الضريبية)
- VAT registration certificate
- Product catalog with pricing
- Reference hotels (if applicable)

### Step 2: AI Verification (2–4 hours)

HotelsVendors' AI agent validates:
- Commercial registry authenticity via government database cross-check
- Tax ID validity and active status
- Product catalog categorization and pricing benchmarking
- Supplier creditworthiness (for factoring eligibility)

### Step 3: ETA Pipeline Configuration (1 hour)

Once verified, the supplier's invoicing pipeline is configured:
- Digital signature certificate installed
- ETA API credentials provisioned
- Invoice template generated with supplier details
- QR code and UUID workflow tested

### Step 4: INVO Marketplace Listing (instant)

The supplier's products go live on INVO — the HotelsVendors supplier marketplace. Hotels can discover, compare, and order immediately.

## Total Time: Under 24 Hours

Most suppliers complete the entire process within one business day. Compare this to traditional marketplace onboarding that takes 2–4 weeks.

## Requirements for Egyptian Suppliers

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Commercial Registry | Active, matching the supplier's business name |
| Tax Card | Valid, with active VAT registration |
| Bank Account | For receiving payments (EGP account required) | 
| Product Catalog | Minimum 10 SKUs with pricing |
| Delivery Capability | Ability to deliver to hotels in their coverage area |

## What Happens After Onboarding

Once live on INVO, suppliers get:
- Access to 500+ verified hotels on the platform
- Automated ETA-compliant invoicing (no manual tax work)
- Optional reverse factoring (get paid in 48 hours)
- Real-time order notifications
- Performance analytics dashboard

## Common Onboarding Issues

**Expired commercial registry**: The most common rejection reason. Suppliers must renew annually.

**Mismatched tax ID**: The tax card must match the commercial registry exactly. Subsidiaries need separate profiles.

**Incomplete catalog**: Suppliers with fewer than 10 SKUs or without pricing get flagged for manual review.
