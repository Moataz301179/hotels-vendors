## Post Body

After 17 years in finance and audit, I kept seeing the same problem:

Hotels in Egypt procure through WhatsApp groups, phone calls, and Excel spreadsheets.

Suppliers chase invoices for 60-90 days.

Nobody knows what anyone else is paying.

So I built HotelsVendors — Egypt's first digital procurement platform for hospitality.

One dashboard. Verified suppliers. Fixed prices. Automated purchase orders.

But the real unlock? Partnering with Oliv Finance.

Now our suppliers don't just get orders — they get paid in 48 hours through Egypt's first FRA-licensed digital factoring platform.

Hotels save. Suppliers grow. The supply chain runs on data.

This is what B2B procurement should have looked like from day one.
