### Why Local Chains — Not International Brands

| Factor | Local Chain (Pickalbatros, Sunrise, Orascom) | International Brand (Marriott, Hilton) |
|--------|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Procurement authority | **Owner/family decides** — can switch in one meeting | HQ procurement policy — 12-18 month vendor onboarding |
| Payment terms | Negotiable per property | Fixed by global contract |
| SKU flexibility | **Willing to experiment** with local suppliers | Locked into approved vendor list |
| Factoring need | **High** — seasonal cash flow crunch | Lower — parent company backstops |
| ETA compliance | Reactive — needs help | Has internal tax team |
| Decision speed | **Days** | Months |

## 4. Seasonal Modeling

### Red Sea Coast — Occupancy & Consumption Curve

| Month | Occupancy | Consumption vs. Annual Avg | Procurement Notes |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| January | 85% | +15% | Peak season — linen turnover highest |
| February | 90% | +20% | Peak — seafood demand max |
| March | 85% | +15% | Spring break surge |
| April | 75% | +5% | Shoulder — prep for low season |
| May | 45% | −40% | **Low season starts** — reduce orders |
| June | 35% | −50% | Lowest occupancy |
| July | 40% | −45% | Ramadan effect (variable) |
| August | 45% | −40% | Local tourism pickup |
| September | 55% | −25% | Pre-season restocking begins |
| October | 75% | +5% | **High season returns** |
| November | 85% | +15% | Peak |
| December | 90% | +20% | Holiday peak — max inventory |

**Key insight:** Coastal properties need **2× inventory in Nov-Feb vs. Jun-Aug**. This creates working capital stress — exactly where factoring helps.

### Cairo — Stability

| Month | Consumption vs. Annual Avg | Notes |
|-------|---------------------------|-------|
| January–March | +5% | Wedding season, conferences |
| April | −10% | Easter / spring break (some closures) |
| May–July | Baseline | Stable |

**Cairo acts as a cash flow stabilizer** for suppliers who serve both coastal and Cairo markets.
