# HotelsVendors Business Model & Pricing Strategy
*QDB Submission Document — July 2026*

## Executive Summary

HotelsVendors is **Egypt's first AI-powered digital procurement infrastructure** for the hospitality sector. We operate a four-sided marketplace connecting **Hotels (Buyers) ↔ Suppliers ↔ Logistics ↔ Factoring Companies** with embedded ETA e-invoicing compliance.

## Revenue Model

### 1. Transaction Fees (Primary)
- **Hotel Buyers:** 1.5% platform fee on completed orders (capped at EGP 50,000/order)
- **Suppliers:** 0.5% listing fee (optional premium placement available)
- **Factoring Partners:** 0.25% facilitation fee on capital deployed

### 2. Subscriptions (Secondary)
- **Supplier Basic:** Free (up to 50 SKUs, 100 orders/month)
- **Supplier Pro:** EGP 2,990/month (unlimited SKUs, priority placement, analytics)
- **Hotel Enterprise:** EGP 4,990/month (multi-property, custom workflows, dedicated CSM)

### 3. ETA Compliance SaaS
- **Bundled:** Included with transaction fees
- **Standalone:** EGP 299/month for off-platform invoices
- **Pay-as-you-go:** EGP 5/invoice (vs competitor 1.25% of invoice value)

### 4. Data Monetization (Deferred)
- Aggregated market intelligence reports (sold to suppliers/funders)
- Pricing benchmarking APIs
