## 3. Procurement Model Analysis

### How Procurement Works in Egyptian Hotels

| Procurement Model | Typical For | Characteristics |
|------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Fully Centralized** | International chains (Accor, Marriott, Hilton, IHG) | Global/regional P2P systems; approved vendor lists; negotiated contracts for F&B, linen, amenities, FF&E; property-level requisitions through HQ systems |
| **Hybrid (Centralized + Local)** | Orascom, Jaz, Pickalbatros, Sunrise | Central procurement office for strategic sourcing and contracts; some local purchasing per property for perishables/urgent needs |
| **Per-Property** | Small independent hotels, owner-operated resorts | Each GM/owner buys independently; no scale benefits; fragmented supply chain |

### Key Insights for B2B Procurement SaaS

1. **International chains** already have mature procurement systems (Accor uses FutureLog for P2P; Marriott uses MGS; IHG has a $4.7B procurement operation). They are **not** your primary target — they are locked into global contracts.

2. **Local chains (Jaz, Orascom, Pickalbatros, Sunrise, Cleopatra, Tolip)** are the **sweet spot**. They manage 15,000+ rooms each, have centralized procurement offices, but likely use fragmented manual processes (spreadsheets, phone orders). A B2B procurement SaaS would solve real pain points.

3. **Egypt-specific procurement challenges**:
   - Coastal resorts (Hurghada, Sharm) are far from Cairo suppliers — logistics cost is high
   - Import dependency for many hotel supplies (linens, electronics, specialty F&B)
   - Currency volatility (EGP devaluation) makes supplier contracts complex
   - ETA e-invoicing compliance is becoming mandatory

4. **Procurement decision-makers** for each chain:
   - **Orascom**: Ghada Mostafa (Central Procurement Hotels Office Head) — CONTACT DIRECTLY
   - **Jaz/Travco**: Group procurement at Sheikh Zayed HQ
   - **Accor Egypt**: Regional procurement via Dubai/Accor global
   - **Marriott Egypt**: Regional via Marriott Middle East office (Dubai)

## 5. Hotel Digitization & Procurement Software News in Egypt

### Key Developments

1. **Foodics + Suplyd Partnership (2023)**: Foodics (restaurant management, MENA) partnered with Suplyd (Egyptian digital procurement platform) to automate procurement/supply chain for restaurants in Egypt. Integration automates ordering, inventory, and supplier management. This signals growing demand for procurement tech in Egyptian hospitality.

2. **HotelsVendors (your project?)**: There is a domain `hotelsvendors.com` described as "Egypt's B2B hospitality procurement infrastructure — AI-automated demand forecasting, embedded reverse factoring, ETA e-invoicing compliance, and shared-route logistics — purpose-built for coastal hotel chains in Sharm El-Sheikh and Hurghada." This is directly relevant to your SaaS.

3. **Government E-Invoicing Mandate (ETA)**: Egypt's Tax Authority (ETA) has mandated e-invoicing for all businesses, including hotels. This creates a compliance driver for hotels to adopt digital procurement systems.

4. **Global Hospitality Procurement Trends**:
   - Consolidation of fragmented systems into unified P2P platforms
   - AI for demand forecasting and inventory management
   - Sustainability tracking in supply chain (Orascom El Gouna is a leader — recycling, plastic hangers, Green Star Award)
   - Mobile-first procurement for property-level staff

5. **Egypt's EGP 50B Subsidized Loan Program**: Hotels can borrow at 12% interest to expand. Many are using this for renovations and new builds — creating a wave of FF&E procurement needs.

6. **FutureLog** (global hospitality P2P leader) has ISAE 3402 certification and is active in MEA. They compete with Zycus, Order.co, and others.
