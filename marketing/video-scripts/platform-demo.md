# HotelsVendors — Platform Sandbox Video Script

> Documentary-style product demo. AI voice (ElevenLabs recommended: "Nova" or "Rachel" — warm British female, 140 wpm). Two cuts: 30s (loop/hero) + 2min (full pitch).
>
> Audience: Resort operations VPs + suppliers selling into Red Sea hotels.
> Tone: Empathetic → authoritative → quietly proud. No hype. No buzzwords.

---

## Full 2-minute cut

### Scene 0 — Cold open (0:00–0:05)

**Visual:** Black screen. Fade in to an aerial drone shot at dawn over the Red Sea. A resort compound — 400 rooms, three pools, a beach restaurant — wakes up.

**Voiceover:**
> "Four hundred rooms. Fifty-six thousand litres of pool water. Three restaurants serving nine hundred covers a night."

**Note:** Let the drone shot breathe for 2s after the VO ends before the cut.

---

### Scene 1 — The supplier's pain (0:05–0:25)

**Visual:** Morning. A supplier's warehouse in Cairo — stacks of linen crates, a desktop with 47 open WhatsApp chats, a PDF invoice pinned to a corkboard dated three months ago. The supplier (mid-40s, tired) stares at a bounced cheque notification on his phone.

**Voiceover:**
> "Four hundred kilometres south, Karim has forty-eight pallets of pool chemicals waiting to ship. His invoice from last Ramadan still hasn't cleared. He's been paid twice this year on time."

> "Across Egypt's hospitality sector, the average payable cycle is ninety-seven days. Suppliers absorb the float. Or they simply stop bidding on resort contracts."

**Note:** Sound design: WhatsApp pings layered over a quiet warehouse hum. No music yet.

---

### Scene 2 — The hotel's pain (0:25–0:50)

**Visual:** Split screen. Left: a procurement manager at a Jaz resort opens her laptop to 14 unread emails about a missing F&B delivery. Right: a compliance officer scrolls through a spreadsheet of ETA invoice QR codes, two of them red-flagged as rejected by the Egyptian Tax Authority.

**Voiceover:**
> "Inside the hotel, Amina is chasing the same suppliers by phone. The weekend buffet at the beach restaurant went short on seafood last Thursday — nobody confirmed the purchase order."

> "And every invoice she files needs to clear ETA validation before it reaches accounts payable. One wrong tax code, and the whole batch rejected at midnight."

**Note:** Subtle ticking-clock sound under the second VO line. Stops cold at the cut to Scene 3.

---

### Scene 3 — The logistics gap (0:50–1:05)

**Visual:** A coastal road at dawn. A Shark-Breaker truck passes a sign reading "Sharm El-Sheikh 87 km." The back is loaded with consolidated crates from four different suppliers.

**Voiceover:**
> "Sharm and Hurghada sit at the end of a long, thin supply chain. Four suppliers, four trucks, four chances to arrive late, with nobody coordinating handoffs. Resorts hold safety stock worth millions — and still run short."

---

### Scene 4 — The platform reveal (1:05–1:15)

**Visual:** The HotelsVendors logo lockup animates onto a clean white background. A single-line dashboard loads: procurement pipeline, invoice status, delivery ETA — all green.

**Voiceover:**
> "HotelsVendors sits between every hotel and every supplier on the Red Sea coast — a single platform for procurement, compliance, and capital."

**Note:** Music kicks in here. Low, ambient, confident. Think Patagonia-doc not tech-ad.

---

### Scene 5 — Solution block (1:15–1:45)

**Visual:** Five modules cascade onto screen, each animated as a clean icon + label:

1. **Marketplace** → a supplier catalogue with fixed-price SKUs
2. **Procurement** → PO automatically generated, ETA-compliant from line 1
3. **Logistics** → Shark-Breaker consolidated coastal delivery with GPS tracking
4. **Factoring** → supplier gets paid in 48h, hotel keeps Net-60 terms
5. **Compliance** → ETA e-invoicing validated in real-time

**Voiceover:**
> "Fixed-price marketplace — no more WhatsApp negotiations over unit costs. Every purchase order ships with ETA-compliant invoicing baked in from click one."

> "Shark-Breaker consolidates coastal logistics into one daily run. Your chef sees every crate, every ETA, on one screen."

> "And when your supplier needs cash before your hotel settles the invoice? Embedded factoring pays them in forty-eight hours — your hotel keeps its Net-sixty terms. Everyone wins."

---

### Scene 6 — The quiet payoff (1:45–1:55)

**Visual:** Sunset drone shot of the same resort from Scene 0. The beach restaurant is full. The water is still. A phone screen shows "All deliveries confirmed — ETA compliant."

**Voiceover:**
> "Four hundred rooms. One platform. Procurement that actually keeps pace with checkout-out time."

---

### Scene 7 — CTA (1:55–2:00)

**Visual:** HotelsVendors URL + "Book a demo" button on clean dark background.

**Voiceover:**
> "HotelsVendors dot com. Book a demo — see your resort's supply chain in sixty seconds."

## Production notes for sandboxing video tool

- **Aspect ratios to render:** 16:9 (YouTube/website), 9:16 (ads/reels), 1:1 (LinkedIn feed)
- **Caption burn-in:** always-on captions for 90% of viewers who watch muted
- **Music track reference:** "Where Is My Mind" instrumental pace (slow piano under VO, swells at Scene 6 sunset). Royalty-free equivalent: search "ambient corporate documentary slow build" on Epidemic Sound.
- **Voiceover talent spec:**
  - Gender: Female
  - Accent: British RP or educated Egyptian-English
  - Age tone: 30–40
  - Pace: ~140 wpm, 20% pause ratio between sentences
  - Recommended ElevenLabs voice IDs: `Rachel` (authoritative), `Bella` (warm/clinical), `Charlotte` (slightly more animated)
- **Brand colours for graphics:** `--accent-base: #C4881F` (warm gold), `--bg-canvas: #FAFAF8` (warm white), text `#1A1816` (warm black)
- **Key icons needed:** Marketplace basket, PO document, truck with wave motif, banknote with clock, ETA shield with QR code
